      <p>
        To make classic potato and cheese perogies from scratch, start by mixing flour, salt, eggs, oil or melted butter, and warm water or sour cream until a smooth, elastic dough forms, then knead it for about five minutes and let it rest covered for 20 to 30 minutes so the gluten relaxes. While the dough rests, boil peeled, cubed baking potatoes in salted water for about 15 minutes until fork-tender, drain them well, and mash them with butter, shredded cheddar cheese, salt, pepper, and optional sautéed onions until smooth, allowing the filling to cool completely before assembly. Once the dough has rested, roll it out on a lightly floured surface to roughly 1/8 inch thick and cut out 3-inch circles using a cookie cutter or glass. Place about one to one-and-a-half tablespoons of cooled filling in the center of each round, fold the dough over into a half-moon, and firmly pinch or crimp the edges shut so no filling escapes during cooking. Cook the assembled perogies in batches in a large pot of boiling salted water for about 2 to 5 minutes until they float to the surface, lift them out with a slotted spoon, and either serve them right away tossed in melted butter or pan-fry them in butter with sautéed onions until golden and crisp on both sides alongside sour cream.
